## Question

Discuss the impact of the Six Vs on data quality in business decision-making. [8 marks]

---

## Worked answer

### Name

The Six Vs framework assesses big data quality across six dimensions: volume, variety, velocity, variability, value, and veracity. Volume, variety, and velocity are commonly identified as the three main factors. Variability, value, and veracity have been added as the gathering and use of big data has grown.

### Explain

Volume measures the sheer amount of data, typically in terabytes or petabytes. Variety covers the range of sources and formats, including structured database records, semi-structured CSV files, and unstructured content such as text, images, and video. Velocity is the speed at which data is generated, transmitted, and processed in real time. Variability is how quickly the data itself changes over the period of analysis. Value is whether the data, after processing, actually meets the predefined goal of the analysis. Veracity is how trustworthy the data is, which typically declines as volume rises.

### Impact

Each V threatens data quality if mishandled, producing a Garbage In, Garbage Out (GIGO) effect: low-quality input on any V produces low-quality decisions regardless of how sophisticated the analysis is. High volume without sufficient storage causes processing bottlenecks. High variety without normalisation produces inconsistent analysis. High velocity without real-time tools means decisions are made on stale data. High variability undermines findings by the time they reach a decision-maker. Low value wastes processing effort. Low veracity destroys confidence in conclusions. Strategic, tactical, and operational decisions all depend on the Six Vs being well-managed; when they are not, decisions misfire and stakeholders lose trust.
